## Evidence and limitations

1. Initial five-step GRPO completed in 9m41s. Last batch reward/advantages were zero;
   all responses hit the 256-token cap. Exit raised a DataLoader worker cleanup error.
2. Explicit resume loaded step-5 model, optimizer, RNG and LR scheduler; ran step 6 and
   wrote a new checkpoint. The same exit cleanup error occurred. Step-6 model state was
   8.5 GiB, optimizer state 357 MiB. Full FSDP checkpoints are not adapter-only exports.
3. The 32 logged rows were 8 real trajectories plus 24 synthetic padding rows. At this
   verl revision actor `ppo_mini_batch_size` is multiplied by rollout.n. Setting it to 8
   requested 32 rows. Setting it to 2 makes the required multiple 8.
4. A fresh, one-update signal smoke used mini-batch 2, dataloader workers 0, response cap
   1024 and max batched tokens 2048. Exactly 8 JSONL rows were observed; exit was clean.
